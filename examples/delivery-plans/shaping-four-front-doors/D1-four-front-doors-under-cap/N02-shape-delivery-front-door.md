## What

As a Shaper invoker, I want `shape:delivery` to be a front door per the accepted IA — correct
directory and symlink names, wrapper regenerated, `using-this-pack` route updated, frontmatter
cut to the surviving fields — so that the door pattern every other fold copies is proven on
the cheapest door first. No content fold here: delivery-shape absorbs nothing; this is the
re-house plus the style pass.

## Why

The bet: prove the seam on the door with zero absorption risk, so N03–N05's failures (if any)
are fold failures, not plumbing failures. Rejected alternative: start with `shape:idea`
(the first door users meet) — that couples the riskiest part (a content fold) to the
first-ever exercise of the renaming plumbing, making failures ambiguous. Unblocks nothing
structurally but de-risks N03–N05's shared mechanics.
