# C3 — Decomposition with framing baked in

**Capability:** turn a fed goal + KRs into the full hierarchy with the framing the user would
otherwise have to re-prompt — acceptance criteria on every story, foundational work surfaced and
folded into the skeleton, the Rule A1 branch honoured, and delegation up/down preserved.
